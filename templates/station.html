Regional hotspot inspector

Compare bright patches on the forehead, nose, cheeks and chin. Outlines are estimated facial regions; amber boxes locate candidate bright patches.

Diagnostic measurements only. These values do not certify a passport photo or change code approval. Near-clipped pixels can indicate lost detail; a darker edited patch does not prove that detail was recovered.

How these measurements work

Bright candidates: at least 80 L* and 15 L* above the regional median, or a colour channel at 250 or above. Near-clipped values count channels at 250–255 in the decoded 8-bit image. These diagnostic thresholds need calibration against shop photographs; they are not country pass limits or Photomatic scores. Eye regions and lips are excluded. Glasses glare needs a separate check. The preview is reduced for display; counts use native image pixels.
