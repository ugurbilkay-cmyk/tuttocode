UK passport photo code

Upload a photo. We check it against HM Passport Office rules, crop it to size and give you a photo code for your online application.

How to take it

  • Ask someone else to take it, arm's length or further away
  • Stand in front of a plain light wall, no shadows
  • Face the camera, plain expression, mouth closed, eyes open
  • Head and shoulders in view with space above your head
  • No glasses if possible, no hat, hair away from eyes
  • No filters, no editing, taken within the last month
